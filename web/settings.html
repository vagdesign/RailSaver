RailSaver

The Swiss railway clock, in 3D.
Clock
Movement & sound
The second hand waits at 12 for the minute impulse. The real clocks wait 1.5 s; 0 = continuous sweep.
Camera & scene
With a sky, the steel and the glass reflect it.
Soft focus of the photo behind the clock, like a camera's depth of field.
Larger = longer shadows under the hands.
Protects OLED and plasma screens from burn-in.
Performance
Above 100% = supersampling (sharper, slower).
Swiss railway clock design by Hans Hilfiker (1944), second hand 1953. RailSaver is not affiliated with SBB CFF FFS or Mondaine.
